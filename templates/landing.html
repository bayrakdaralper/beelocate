About & trust

BeeLocate PRO is built by a GIS + design team with academic research experience. The system combines satellite vegetation signals (NDVI), climate summaries, terrain, and other spatial indicators to provide a structured, repeatable assessment.

Methodology note: NDVI is a vegetation density indicator (not plant species). Climate metrics are long-term reanalysis summaries. BeeLocate PRO is a decision-support tool and does not guarantee outcomes.
