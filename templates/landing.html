Frequently Asked Questions

Do you guarantee honey yield?

No. While our data helps you identify the best potential locations based on vegetation and climate, nature is unpredictable. We provide decision support, not yield guarantees.

Does this work in my country?

Try your exact location for free—if it looks off, don’t buy the report. Data availability varies by region. BeeLocate Pro uses global satellite datasets, but vegetation indices may vary in accuracy in extremely arid or snow-covered regions.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required. BeeLocate Pro runs entirely in your web browser. You can access your reports from any device.

How do I get my report after paying?

Once payment is confirmed, the full analysis runs immediately. You will be redirected to the results page where you can download your PDF report instantly. A copy is also sent to your email.
